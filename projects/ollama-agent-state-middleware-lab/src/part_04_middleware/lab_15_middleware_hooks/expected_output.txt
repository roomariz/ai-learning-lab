Middleware hook phases
======================

BEFORE_REQUEST:
Called before any checks run.

CHECK (MIDDLE):
Validation or authorisation logic.

AFTER_REQUEST:
Called after the request is handled.

ON_ERROR:
Called when an exception propagates.

Conclusion
==========

Middleware hooks separate lifecycle concerns: before, during, after, and on_error. This makes it easier to add logging, metrics, or cleanup without mixing it into business logic.
